# Brewsy ☀️

Your news, brewed overnight. One calm edition every morning: the stories that matter in the
topics you pick (AI, tech, business, world, politics, science, health, climate, oceans, cars,
gaming, sports, entertainment, movies and crypto), explained simply, with as much depth as you want.

- **Today** — the whole edition in one scroll. Swipe left/right (or the Today/Yesterday
  switch) to change day; the calendar at the end of that switch opens older editions.
  A row of chips under the cover (Latest, Summary, each of your topics, On the radar, Markets)
  jumps straight to a section, and the chip for the section you're reading lights up. Past the
  cover the row pins to the top with **search** and **settings**, so both are always one tap
  away; the list icon opens "In this edition" with every section and what's in it.
  Each topic section holds its full stories and then its quick hits ("More in AI"). After your
  topics comes a short **local section** named after where you chose (India, the United States,
  the United Kingdom, Canada or Australia): one or two local stories and a few one-liners, made at
  5 am there. Everyone shares the world edition; if today's isn't out yet where you are, Today
  shows the latest one with a quiet note.
  Each story has three layers: the short version → **Learn more** (explained simply,
  background, key players, key terms, what to watch, earlier stories about the same people and
  companies, all sources) → **Go deeper** (a longer explainer written from the story's sources)
  plus **Ask a question about this** (each phone gets a few of these a day). Every story can be
  played, shared or saved.
- **Your topics** — star up to 4 of the topics you follow for **more of this**: they come first
  on Today, get 4–6 full stories where the news supports it and more quick hits, and lead the
  audio briefing. **Movies** covers Hollywood, Bollywood and the other Indian film industries,
  plus world cinema (East Asia, the Philippines, the Middle East, Turkey, Africa, Europe,
  Australia, Canada), animation, documentaries and indie film (box office, releases, trailers,
  casting, festivals, awards). **Oceans** (off until you switch it on) covers sea life, the deep
  sea, ocean science, fisheries and the health of the seas.
  Under an opened story, **Stories like this: More · Less** tunes it further; "Less" can hide a
  person, company or word (Settings → Hidden words).
- **Search** — the last two weeks of editions, offline over saved ones. A result opens on Today.
- **Reading List** — tap a bookmark to save a story for Tonight / This weekend / Later this
  week / No rush (long-press saves it for no rush). Gentle reminders, never more than one a day.
  Opened stories get a small check; the end of the edition shows how many you opened and a quiet
  days-in-a-row count (kept on the phone).
- **Listen** — **▶ Listen · 8 min** under the date plays the edition as a radio-style
  briefing: Quick (~3 min) or Full (~10 min), one chapter per story, read by the phone's own voice
  in your own running order: your starred topics first, only the topics you follow, without
  stories you've hidden, then your local section. A mini player sits above
  the tab bar; tap it for chapters, speed (0.8×–2×), offline download and **Driving mode**
  (huge buttons, screen stays on). Every story has a small ▶ to start from it, and the
  Reading List has **Play my reading list**. Plays with the screen locked in an installed build.
- **Never miss the big one** — ~150 sources (wires, major outlets, tech/AI, specialist press for
  each beat including film trades, Indian cinema and world-cinema press, official sources, Yahoo
  News as a safety net),
  grouped and scored; anything 5+ major outlets cover is always in, and every topic with real news
  gets at least one full story. During the day Brewsy checks every 2 hours and adds **Since this
  morning** at the top, with an optional breaking-news push (at most 2 a day). **How this briefing
  was made** at the bottom shows what was scanned.
- **Settings** — the gear on Today and the Reading List (also in the pinned bar): theme, topics
  and stars, reading (text size, hidden words), wake-up time, **news from** (your local section),
  breaking news, listening, sources (world outlets plus your region's; turning one off drops its
  links, and any story only it reported), reminders, PDF export, past editions, and Help →
  **Take the tour again**.
- **First run** — welcome → pick your look → where your news comes from (picked from the phone's
  timezone, then its region setting) → topics (with stars) → wake-up time → notifications,
  then a short tour of Today that points at the real controls (Skip or Android back ends it).
- **Themes** — twenty complete looks, each with its own typefaces, shapes, card style, section
  headings and cover, in four groups (Settings → Theme, or onboarding):
  - **Classic:** **Glass** (frosted panes on a soft wallpaper, after Apple's Liquid Glass; Light,
    Dark, or Auto = light from 7 am to 7 pm), **Space** (black, monospace details, Earthset on the
    cover, topics open on numbered photo cards), **Atlantis** (deep-blue gradient, serif, round
    frosted cards, a kelp forest on the cover, a depth gauge that reads deeper with each topic),
    **Highlands** (forest tiles, bold italics, Buachaille Etive Mòr on the cover, each topic opens at
    a waymarker), **Alpine** (white, Swiss red, the Matterhorn on the cover, topics open on a Swiss
    grid). Their accents stay small: the stories tile is a card with its count in the accent
    (Alpine keeps its red block, Highlands uses a deep moss). Each also has a few small lines of its
    own (the end of the edition, empty states).
  - **Films:** each cover is a real photograph of a place or thing that recalls the film, graded to
    the theme. **Dune** (the dunes of Sossusvlei, thin wide-set capitals, centered), **Matrix** (a
    VT100's screen in phosphor green, monospace, sections open on a `> prompt_`), **Tron** (a night
    interchange in cyan light trails, light-cycle trails under each title), **Blade Runner** (a
    rainy, lantern-lit Tokyo alley, amber haze, neon pink), **Budapest** (the Grandhotel Pupp washed
    pastel pink, perfect symmetry, crossed keys over each section), **Star Wars** (the Tunisian salt
    flat where Tatooine was filmed, at dusk, crawl-yellow capitals, sections open as "Episode IV"),
    **Hogwarts** (Gloucester Cathedral's candlelit cloisters, warm stone, gold and crimson, sections
    open like book chapters), **La La Land** (Griffith Observatory at magic hour, a yellow accent,
    sections numbered like a programme), **Ghibli** (summer clouds over the hills of Biei), **Fight
    Club** (a bare bulb in the dark, typewriter type, each section a reel with a cue mark in its
    corner). Each film hides a few nods to itself: its own lines in empty and end-of-edition
    states, and details in its section headings (some only on some days).
  - **Horror:** **Nosferatu** (silent film, blackletter weekday, intertitle cards), **The Ring**
    (worn VHS, a PLAY counter on each section), **Midsommar** (endless daylight, embroidered
    section borders), **Scream** (black, white and blood red, a cordless phone ringing in a dark
    kitchen, sharp serif capitals, each topic opens with one of the rules), **Insidious** (a
    lamp-lit dark, thin Edwardian capitals, each topic opens under a red door that stands a little
    further open as you read). Each hides a few nods to its film in its cover, section headings
    and small lines (the end of the edition, empty states).
  - **Custom:** **Whales** (a sunlit sea with a small pod of whales drifting slowly behind the
    page, round Fredoka titles, white cards, a coral accent, a deep navy stories tile; the whales
    hold still when the phone asks for less motion).

  Defined in `src/theme/themes.ts` (classic) and `src/theme/packs/` (glass, whales, films, horror),
  with per-theme section headings in `src/components/headings/` and the Whales pod in
  `src/components/ambient/`; covers in `assets/themes/` (the classic and film covers are
  photographs, credited in Settings → Theme; the horror, Glass and Whales covers are original art). Only the current
  theme's typefaces load at startup; the rest load in the background.
- **Logo** — a coffee bean with an S-shaped cut, drawn in thin line in each theme's ink. The
  launcher icon is the same bean, filled: roast brown with an amber cut on espresso. Sources are
  in `assets/brand/` (icons only change with a new EAS build, not over the air).

## Run it

```bash
npm install
npx expo start          # scan the QR code with Expo Go on your phone
```

Without a backend the app shows a bundled sample edition (7 Oct 2026), so you can try
everything except Go deeper / Ask and the notifications.

Expo Go can't play audio with the screen locked, show lock-screen controls, receive pushes
on Android or add home-screen shortcuts. For those, install a build:
[docs/ANDROID_BUILD.md](docs/ANDROID_BUILD.md) (it also says how to test them).

## The backend

Supabase (free plan) + Claude (or Gemini's free tier) to write the edition + OpenAI or
ElevenLabs for the studio voice. Costs and setup: [docs/BACKEND_SETUP.md](docs/BACKEND_SETUP.md).
Pushes and lock-screen audio need an installed build: [docs/ANDROID_BUILD.md](docs/ANDROID_BUILD.md).

## Where things are

```
src/app/            screens: onboarding, (tabs)/index = Today, (tabs)/reading-list, search
src/components/     UI pieces (StoryView, LearnMore, GoDeeper, sheets, glass, jump bar, tour/, …)
src/audio/          the audio player: sessions/chapters, engine, downloads, saved position
src/state/          on-device data: settings, reading list, cached explainers, read marks
src/theme/          tokens, the classic themes (themes.ts) and the theme packs (packs/)
src/data/           edition loading + caching, API calls, the sample edition, types
src/lib/            notifications, PDF export, formatting, curation (what Today shows), search
supabase/           the backend: database migrations + edge functions
plugins/            config plugins (Android home-screen shortcuts)
assets/brand/       the logo's source SVGs and the app icon set
```

Web preview flags (development): `?onboarded=1` (also skips the tour) · `?tour=0|1` ·
`?theme=<name>` · `?glass=light|dark|auto` · `?blur=0` (draws glass the way Android does) ·
`?seed=1` (sample Reading List) · `?preview=loading|empty|error` ·
`?region=IN|CA|US|UK|AU|WORLD` · `?local=sample` (a sample local section) · `?fallback=1`
(today's edition "not out yet") · `?audio=parts` (a script in parts, so Listen builds your own
running order).

## Voice assistants, CarPlay and Android Auto

- **Siri:** open the Shortcuts app → **+** → add the **Open URLs** action with
  `brewsy://listen` (or `brewsy://listen?mode=quick`) → name it "Play my briefing". Then
  "Hey Siri, play my briefing" starts it. A built-in Siri phrase (no setup) needs App Intents,
  native Swift code in a custom development build; it isn't in this app yet.
- **Google Assistant / Android:** long-press the Brewsy icon for **Listen to briefing** and
  **Quick listen** (drag one to the home screen for one tap). "Hey Google, open Brewsy" works;
  a spoken "play my briefing" command would need Google's App Actions (a `shortcuts.xml`
  capability plus Play Console review).
- **Lock screen, Bluetooth and car:** play/pause and the ±10 buttons work everywhere; in Brewsy
  the ±10 buttons move to the previous/next story. Bluetooth and car next/previous buttons
  aren't wired to stories, because expo-audio only offers ±10s seek commands.
- **Full CarPlay / Android Auto apps** (a Brewsy screen in the car with a chapter list) need:
  a native media module (react-native-track-player v5, which is licensed, or custom native
  code), Apple's CarPlay audio entitlement (requested from Apple), an Android
  `MediaBrowserService` + Android Auto metadata, and a development build. Until then, Brewsy
  shows up in the car like any audio app playing through Bluetooth/USB.
